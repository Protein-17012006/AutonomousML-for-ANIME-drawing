\section{Frontend implementation}
The frontend uses Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and shadcn/ui. The public landing page is statically renderable, while the protected \texttt{/copilot} route loads the browser-only workspace client-side.

The workspace is designed for media submission, live QA feedback, artist review, and session-oriented interaction. Specific delivered frontend work is listed in the Progress section.
